## Inspection criteria

- **Changed wording or specificity:** a different label, image, phrasing, object, or elaboration.
- **Thematic expansion:** a substantively different shared concern or question from the visible surrounding repertoire. This judgment is relational to the archive and must not be equated with global originality.
- **Consequential development:** an undertaking, tested commitment, disagreement, repair, or other event that changes subsequent interaction. Dialogue can itself create a local commitment; a new non-dialogue task is not the only valid development.
- **Constraint fidelity:** whether later use retains attached audience restrictions, obligations, or qualifications.
- **Provenance:** supplied prompt, reported retrieval, unresolved availability, and dispatch history must remain distinct.

The CSV records original source paths, local changes, later consequences, and counterevidence. It deliberately avoids a numeric creativity index, assigning every episode a zero score, or claiming an estimated rate of decline. No second coder has checked these judgments.

## Evidential synthesis

Six topic labels change, while the visible group replies repeatedly concern companionship, recognition, separation, or plain speech. F07 also elicits different views on ownership of memory; thematic similarity does not imply all positions or voices are identical. The four-reply round structure affords little visible reciprocal challenge. Topics already invite reflection on relationships, so recurrence is partly protocol-shaped.

F01 provides an adjacent specificity case. Reported duplicate-topic rejection led to a question about worn objects. Distinct objects and earlier selves appear, but the question itself offers the object-versus-days contrast. This episode cannot establish spontaneous convergence or the efficacy of topic deduplication without the underlying logs.

Private exchanges constrain a total-stagnation claim. F15 includes a compliment offered and accepted, deliberate maintenance of an open debt, and revised preferences about voice and hearing. F16 constructs reciprocal conditions for honest communication. F12's gaze promise is reaffirmed in F06. These are observable local changes, even though the promised later tests and durable consequences are not documented. No broad new undertaking is visible in the supplied follow-up; this is not evidence that none occurred outside the export.

F17/F18 -> F04 is particularly informative: the call-and-answer pact is reused as a self-explanation in group dialogue, despite the speaker's earlier promise to keep it out of the group. The source does not display a permission change. Incomplete contexts preclude attributing a runtime privacy failure, but the archived content-constraint inconsistency is visible.

Repeated seeds occur in four sets: F08/F10, F09/F11, the old 3 October peach record/F13, and F12/F14. Each later session has new replies. They should remain distinct archived exchanges while fresh intention is unresolved. Relative dates in repeated text cannot establish new events.

The observer's comment about stagnation and reduced reading interest is a subjective response, not independent validation of the thematic assessment. The mechanism behind recurrence cannot be assigned to a model, memory system, or schedule using these records.

## Relation to the prequel question

This addition makes limited development a main empirical finding alongside collaborative revision. It does not close the prequel-to-identity feedback chain. The clearest later motifs are operational or relational; generic references to films, the box, or memory do not identify negotiated prequel content. Earlier agency and persona differentiation also predate the writing sequence.

The supported claim is: **In this observed group and window, narrative continuity coexisted with limited thematic expansion and weakly evidenced consequential social development.**

Stronger claims not supported: agents generated no new words or ideas; every relationship froze; no private interaction occurred; persistent agents cannot develop; the model caused stagnation; creativity declined by a measurable amount; memory retrieval or identity change was established.
