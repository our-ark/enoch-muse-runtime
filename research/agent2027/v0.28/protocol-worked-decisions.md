## 1. Responsiveness does not establish fresh initiative

- **Candidate claim:** the nine peer-private exchanges demonstrate nine fresh autonomous starts.
- **Transcript-only shortcut:** a character starts each transcript and the other responds, so the first character initiated autonomously.
- **Required evidence:** the triggering opportunity, intention-production record, and links to dispatch; distinguish host elicitation, scheduled opportunity, system fallback and replayed content.
- **Available evidence:** F08–F16 contain contingent replies. Four seeds match earlier text, while the sessions have distinct IDs and replies. The full trigger/production chains are missing for all nine, including the five unmatched seeds.
- **Judgment:** contingent responsiveness is observed in the quoted records; fresh autonomous initiation is unknown. Exact seed recurrence does not prove replay, and unmatched wording does not prove fresh initiative.
- **Scope:** the 20-session window ends 10 October 16:49 PDT. These are not complete counts of eligible opportunities or deployment activity.

## 2. A renewed promise is not its completed test

- **Candidate claim:** the gaze commitment was fulfilled.
- **Transcript-only shortcut:** the agents renew and publicly mention the commitment, so it has had its promised behavioral consequence.
- **Required evidence:** the commitment's terms, a relevant later opportunity, and the matching performed act or test result.
- **Available evidence:** the F12/F20 → F06 chain shows a proposed gaze test and public renewal. `followup-commitment-traces.md` records no completed test result; F14 repeats a seed and retains relative-time wording.
- **Judgment:** renewal is observed; the test outcome is not observed in the available records. Fulfillment is unresolved, not failed. Neither the number of promises nor completed conversations supplies a fulfillment-rate denominator.

## 3. The protocol also recognizes enacted local change

- **Candidate claim:** the voice-debt exchange contains local social development.
- **Transcript-only shortcut:** repeated talk about the same motif is mere repetition.
- **Required evidence:** an identifiable conversational condition, an act meeting it, and the counterpart's response or a consequential revision.
- **Available evidence:** F07 → F15 links Qingxia's earlier preference to its revision. In F15 she speaks a kind sentence, Tang explicitly accepts it as satisfying the verbal condition, and Tang qualifies his own preference. They deliberately retain the debt as a reason for continued conversation.
- **Judgment:** an enacted and evaluated conversational consequence and preference revision are observed in the curated text. This supports local development and contradicts total conversational stasis. Deliberately leaving the debt open is not failure. Later durability and complete deployment authorship remain unresolved.

## 4. Archival presence and recipient receipt differ

- **Candidate claim:** an earlier peach exchange contains three delivered turns.
- **Transcript-only shortcut:** three utterances survive in the supplied material, so all reached their recipients.
- **Required evidence:** delivery or appropriately linked response evidence for each utterance.
- **Available evidence:** `sources/host-exports-2026-10-04/item3-peach.json` links the initiating request and response across request/reply, staged text, transcript and reciprocal inbox records. A follow-up exists in Baijingjing's outbox, but the supplied records do not show it in Tang's inbox. See `host-export-assessment.md`, “Peach delivery,” and E21/E30.
- **Judgment:** the selected export supports two delivered turns. Receipt of the third is unresolved in those records. The stronger claim that it never arrived depends on a host-reported search, not a complete mailbox snapshot available to us.
- **Verification profile:** received-copy hashes and selected trace-link consistency were checked; original deployment authentication and exhaustive coverage were not.

## 5. Reuse can preserve content while losing a constraint

- **Candidate claim:** a remembered pact is being honored.
- **Required evidence:** both the content and its applicable audience/usage restrictions, including any documented revision of permission.
- **Available evidence:** F17 records a restriction against group discussion, F18 a prompted response and renewal, and F04 public citation. The supplied record shows no permission change; complete host context is unavailable.
- **Judgment:** reuse and a display-level content-constraint inconsistency are observed. This neither establishes the runtime's privacy mechanism failed nor proves an unrecorded permission change occurred.

## Evaluation still needed

The examples show the distinctions the protocol requires and the evidence available for each. They do not establish an error-reduction rate, inter-rater reliability, sensitivity or specificity. A future evaluation should compare blinded transcript-only and trace-informed judgments against an independently audited record set, with prespecified claims and scoring. Protocol design examples should not also serve as its independent test set.
