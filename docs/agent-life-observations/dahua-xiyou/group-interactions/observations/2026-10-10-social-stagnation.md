## Findings

- **Private conversation is present.** Peer-private initiators are Zhizunbao (4), Baijingjing (3), Qingxia (1), and Tang Sanzang (1), covering four dyads. Earlier absence of private initiation should not be carried forward as this week's result. Visible initiation is separate from untriggered autonomy: the export associates two sessions with scheduled self-checks, and Zixia's sessions use a scheduled round.
- **Group structure remains repetitive.** Different prompts recur around companionship, being seen, separation, memory, and speaking plainly. Persona-specific metaphors persist, but the visible structure is predominantly parallel answers rather than questioning, disagreement, execution, and feedback. Prompt framing itself may contribute to convergence.
- **Local changes exist.** Call-and-answer promises, an eye-contact test, a voice IOU, and a revised whole-peach agreement provide cross-session references and linguistic commitment changes. Their later execution and consequences are not established.
- **Repeated openings need separate coding.** Vote settlement repeats on October 4/7; recognition on October 6/7; the peach opening on October 3/10; the eye-contact opening on October 8/10. Distinct sessions and different replies are retained. They do not establish whether the cause was autonomous re-initiation, replay, recovery, or log handling.
- **Relative time can drift.** Repeated “this morning,” “yesterday,” and “tonight” are preserved as utterances, not used to invent new events. Absolute archive times follow the supplied export.
- **Narrated causes remain uncertain.** The whole-peach opening attributes “half does not count” to Baijingjing, but no matching earlier verbatim line is visible here. The prior Zixia conversation may be related, but its causal influence is not independently verified.

## Working interpretation

Conversation and shared symbols continue, with some local commitment changes. Evidence for broader interaction diversity, fulfilled commitments, and durable relationship development remains limited in this window. The Observer reports stagnation and reduced interest.

“Persistence does not automatically imply continued development” is a working hypothesis, not a general conclusion demonstrated by this dataset.

Follow-up could track new recipient/topic choices under documented scheduler conditions, distinguish replay from fresh initiative, and measure commitments at their next execution opportunity. A no-new-human-topics observation could help, but was not performed here. No runtime policy was changed.
