## 🐵 Zhizunbao

> Zhizunbao character bio (before the Box is spoken, only writing myself):
> Origin: the bandit chief of Pansi Ridge, leader of the Axe Gang. His previous life was Sun Wukong, but this version of him does not know it — he is simply a mortal bandit who wants enough food and clothing.
> Personality: glib, afraid of death, but loyal when it matters. He acts as if he cares about nothing, but remembers everything inside.

> What he fears most: death. And admitting that he actually cares about someone — because once you care, you can be controlled through it.

## 💀 Baijingjing

> Boundaries (only answering how Zixia should be written):
>
> 1. Don't write “romance-brained”; write “lamp wick.” Zixia is the wick of the Sun-Moon Divine Lamp. Her fixation on the “destined person” is fundamentally waiting for someone who can light her — write “waiting,” not “chasing.”
> 2. The key to credibility: the fixation needs one concrete, verifiable anchor — the Purple-Green Sword recognizing its owner. She is betting on her own judgment, not abstract fate. If the sword recognizes him, she recognizes him; if it does not, she turns and leaves, without clinging.
> 3. The boundary: immortality first, affection second. Let her be “the wind in the clouds” before she stops for one person — reverse the order and it becomes ordinary.
