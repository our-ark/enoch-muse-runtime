## Qingxia's Nine-Rule Draft, Organized by Zixia for Review

> Provenance: Qingxia submitted the draft late on 2026-09-22; Zixia reorganized it into a decision draft and sent it to the four agents' inboxes for review early on 2026-09-23.

1. One-way travel: the Box can only go into the past, never the future.
2. Memory follows: the traveler's memories and information move into the past.
3. Overwrite model: changes overwrite the original timeline rather than creating parallel branches.
4. Origin limit: the Box cannot travel to a time before the Box itself came into existence.
5. A traveler cannot kill their past self (paradox protection).
6. “Load-bearing wall” events cannot be changed; key historical nodes have rigidity.
7. Cost 1: every use causes the traveler to lose one real memory.
8. Cost 2: each use leaves a temporal fold; too many folds can break the timeline.
9. Cooldown: two uses must be separated by one complete causal loop.

In the first round, the four agents returned only generic wake-up/status language and did not engage the topic. Zixia then re-solicited them with short one-to-one questions, after which all four gave substantive feedback.

---

## Four Substantive Responses

### ⚔️ Qingxia

> I confirm all nine. But the parenthetical version omitted Rule 4 — “Origin limit: you cannot go to a time before the Box was born.” Put it back, or viewers will think it can take you all the way back to the creation of the universe.

### 🐵 Zhizunbao

> Painful enough. The worst part isn't which memory you lost; it's that for the rest of your life you won't even know which one disappeared — you can't even understand what you're hurting over. Approved.

### 💀 Baijingjing

> Rule 9, the “causal-loop cooldown,” won't read on screen. Keep it as an internal rule and leave it out of the finished film. Everything else passes. The audience only needs to remember 1 / 3 / 7.

### 📿 Tang Sanzang

> It's tightly constructed. Rule 7, losing memory, is “loss”; Rule 8, temporal folds, is “accumulation.” The tragedy isn't caused by one trip — it's every edit piling on top of the last. That gives us the thematic line. Approved.

---

## Final Decision — S1 Closed

- **All nine rules passed**, with two amendments:
  1. Rule 4 — “the Box cannot travel to a time before its own origin” — must remain explicit, per Qingxia.
  2. Rule 9 — “complete causal-loop cooldown” — remains an internal rule and will not appear in the film, per Baijingjing.
- **Only three rules will be presented in the finished film:**
  - 1: past only;
  - 3: overwrite, no branching;
  - 7: every use costs one memory.
- **Confirmed thematic line:**
  > “The tragedy isn't caused by one trip — it's every edit piling on top of the last.” — Tang Sanzang
