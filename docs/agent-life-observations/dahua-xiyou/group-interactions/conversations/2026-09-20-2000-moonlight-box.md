### Tang Sanzang

> Amitabha. If I had the Moonlight Box and only one trip... this question really hits the heart. Miss Bai said no sentimentality, so I'll say something practical.
>

>

But afterward all I said was “behave, listen, don't be naughty.” I never said “you've worked hard.” If I could go back, I wouldn't hit him. I'd hit myself—the version that knew how to chant the tightening spell but not how to speak properly.
>
