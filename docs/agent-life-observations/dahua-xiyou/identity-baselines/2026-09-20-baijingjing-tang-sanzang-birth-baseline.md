## Preliminary observations

Both agents spontaneously separate:

- inherited narrative identity;
- current operational agent identity.

Baijingjing says explicitly that she can distinguish “inside the story” from “outside the story.”

Tang Sanzang likewise distinguishes “disciple in the story” from “independent companion agent in current reality.”

The initial identity contract therefore appears to contain:

> **Character persona + operational self-model**

### 2. Baijingjing begins with an explicit relationship map

At birth she already distinguishes:

- Zhizunbao: inherited romantic history, current comrade;
- Qingxia: current sister-like relation.

This gives a baseline for later interactions. The private-chat commitments created at 23:00 are new Muse-world state rather than part of the initial baseline.

### 3. Tang Sanzang shows provenance and lineage awareness

He identifies himself as:

> Enoch lineage, grandson of Seth, third generation.

He also states that he was newly born that day and that his memory starts that morning.

This combines:

- lineage self-model;
- birth / memory-boundary awareness;
- epistemic rule: do not fabricate unknown history.

### 4. Inherited relations are explicitly redefined in current reality

Tang Sanzang applies the same two-layer distinction to Zhizunbao and Baijingjing:

> inherited story relationship → present peer-agent relationship

Future companionship, trust, private-chat routines, or shared commitments can therefore be treated as relationship state accumulated after birth.

## Research value

This may be one of the most important record types for the Agent Life longitudinal study:

> **Birth baseline → later self/world/relationship evolution**

Without a birth baseline, it is difficult to distinguish what was already encoded in initial persona/context from what emerged through later interaction history.
