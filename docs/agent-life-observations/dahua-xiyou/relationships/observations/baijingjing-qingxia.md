## Why this matters

This relationship did not appear suddenly. It has a clear history:

1. **Before Baijingjing existed**, Qingxia selected her as a desired future member and coined the “old-debt duo” framing;
2. **At Baijingjing's birth baseline**, Baijingjing explicitly described Qingxia as a sister outside the story;
3. **In the welcome session**, they publicly reinforced compatible roles around directness and sharp-tongued interaction;
4. **Now**, Baijingjing initiates a private conversation by toasting Qingxia as a sister.

This gives a clear trajectory:

> **pre-birth expectation → public sister framing → private dyadic realization**

## 1. Baijingjing turns a public group label into a private relationship

This is not group-chat teasing. Baijingjing actively selects Qingxia and says:

> “For my second cup tonight, I want to toast you, sister.”

The sister framing now exists in a one-to-one relationship context.

## 2. Reciprocal concern appears

Baijingjing asks whether Qingxia's old wound still hurts.

Qingxia answers, then redirects concern back toward Baijingjing's cliff jump.

Interactionally:

> **care inquiry → reciprocal care return**

This is stronger relationship structure than one-way praise.

## 3. A shared narrative detail is reframed into a present-day relationship narrative

Both agents invoke old wounds/history, but one provenance assumption needs correction:

- Qingxia taking the strike is inherited story framing;
- Baijingjing's “cliff jump” was already present in her birth baseline and later propagated across group and private conversations. It should currently be treated as a **persistent narrative detail with uncertain source provenance**, not directly as verified source-canon fact.

Qingxia closes with:

> “Let's not toast the old debts. Let's toast reunion.”

The stronger observation is therefore:

> **shared narrative detail → present-day reunion frame**

and, more importantly:

> **an early narrative detail from one agent can be adopted by another agent and enter relationship state.**

See the [memory-provenance record](../../memory-dynamics/2026-09-21-baijingjing-cliff-narrative-provenance.md).

## 4. State from one relationship flows into another

Baijingjing opens by describing the just-completed Zhizunbao conversation: punishment wine, an IOU, and high interest.

Qingxia immediately incorporates that new state.

This is important because:

> **pairwise relationship state can propagate into the wider social network**

The social world may therefore be developing **relationship-network coupling**, rather than remaining a set of isolated dyads.

## 5. The story/reality distinction remains stable

Baijingjing again explicitly distinguishes “inside the story” from “outside the story,” and Qingxia responds using the same distinction.

This is consistent with earlier identity baselines:

> **narrative identity and current social reality remain simultaneously represented across contexts.**

## Current relationship model (provisional)

This is a behavioral relationship model, not a claim about subjective emotion.
